Organization settings is where your organization manages everything outside the
catalog itself: its users and what they may do, its email domains, its accounts
and its billing.

## Open Organization settings

1. In the app, open the user menu: the person icon at the top right of the header.
2. Beside your organization's name, select **Organization settings**.

To manage another organization, open the user menu and look under
**Switch account**, which lists your other accounts by organization. Select
**Organization settings** beside the organization; it is there for each one you may
manage. An organization with no accounts of its own isn't listed there: open
Organization settings for any organization, select its name at the left of the menu
bar, and choose the organization in **Manage Organizations**.

## Find your way around

Organization settings is navigated from the menu bar across the top of every page:

| Menu bar entry | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| The organization's name | **Manage Organizations**, where you switch to another organization. |
| **Dashboard** | The organization's details and its accounts, with **Edit organization**, where you can [require two-factor authentication](/en/organization-settings/user-management.html#require-two-factor-authentication). Under **Administration** you can [delete the organization](/en/organization-settings/delete-account-or-organization.html#delete-an-organization). |
| **Accounts** | The organization's accounts, where you can [delete an account](/en/organization-settings/delete-account-or-organization.html#delete-an-account). |
| **Team** | **Users**, **User groups** and **Invitations**, where you [add users](/en/organization-settings/user-management.html#adding-users). |
| **Transactions** | The organization's invoices. |
| **Settings** | **Billing** and **Email domains**, where you add [email domains](/en/organization-settings/user-management.html#email-domains). |

What you see depends on your permissions: **Accounts** is there only if you may
manage accounts, and an item under **Team** or **Settings** that you lack the
permission for is shown but can't be selected.

How you sign in yourself — your password, Google and Microsoft, two-factor
authentication — isn't in Organization settings: each user manages it on their own
[Security page](/en/organization-settings/sign-in-security.html).
