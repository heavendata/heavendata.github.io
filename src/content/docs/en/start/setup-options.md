After sign-up, heavendata asks how to set up your account and offers three options: **Empty setup**, **Standard setup** and **Managed setup**. Standard setup gives you one product type that fits any kind of product and opens the list of ready-made product types, so you can add or import products right away.

| Option | What it does | Choose it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Empty setup** | Creates nothing. You create every attribute, section and product type yourself. | You have a clear picture of your attributes and how they should be organized. |
| **Standard setup** | Creates the product type *General products* with 14 attributes, then opens **New product type** so you can add ready-made product types for the kinds of product you sell. | You want to add or import products right away and grow the structure as you go. |
| **Managed setup** | Opens a contact page. You send us an email and our team sets the account up for you. | You want us to do the setup. |

## Empty setup

With Empty setup you build the catalog structure yourself: first the attributes and sections under **Settings → Attributes & sections**, then the product types under **Settings → Product types**. Every product has a product type, so you need at least one before you can add a product. See [First steps](./first-steps.html) for the order to do it in.

## Standard setup

Standard setup creates one product type, **General products**, and opens the **New product type** page with the note *Your account starts with one product type, "General products". Create a product type for each kind of product you sell, with the attributes that kind needs.*

General products fits any kind of product. It brings [the 13 attributes every ready-made product type brings](./ready-made-product-types.html#what-every-ready-made-product-type-brings) plus *Target gender*, with the [roles](/en/concepts/attribute-roles.html) an import needs already assigned, and one variant configuration, *No product variants*.

From the **New product type** page, create a ready-made product type for each kind of product you sell: *Tops & shirts*, *Sofas & armchairs*, *Smartphones* and so on. Each one reuses the attributes General products already created and adds the ones its kind of product needs. The full list is on [Ready-made product types](./ready-made-product-types.html); the steps are on [Product types](/en/concepts/product-types.html#create-a-product-type-from-a-ready-made-one).

The names are created in English or German, depending on your account's languages ([which language](/en/concepts/product-types.html#which-language-a-ready-made-product-type-is-created-in)). Standard setup is refused with *Your catalog already has data. Delete product types and attributes first.* when the account already has an attribute or a section.

Accounts set up before 21 October 2026 got the product type *Simple product* instead. Its attributes are reused by the ready-made product types in the same way.

## Managed setup

With Managed setup you send us an email, we contact you, and we set the account up for you. That typically takes about 24 hours.
