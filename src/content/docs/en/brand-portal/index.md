The brand portal is a separate, branded website where partners and resellers
browse your published products and download product data, images and files —
instead of waiting for you to send a file. It has no login: anyone who has its
address can open it, so its settings, not invitations, decide what it shows.

[Brand portal settings](/en/brand-portal/overview.html) covers
[who can see your brand portal](/en/brand-portal/overview.html#who-can-see-your-brand-portal),
what each setting shares, the downloads and the portal's languages.
